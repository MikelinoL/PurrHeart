# PurrHeart

**A little purr. A quiet moment.**

PurrHeart is a minimal iPhone app that brings natural cat purring to a quiet break in your day.

[Download PurrHeart on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6837288620) · [Support](https://purrheart-support.michaellyubarskiy.chatgpt.site/) · [Privacy policy](https://purrheart-support.michaellyubarskiy.chatgpt.site/privacy.html)

## What it does

- Touch one central paw button to start listening.
- Keep your finger down and slide to set a timer from **5 to 30 minutes**, in five-minute steps.
- Tap again to stop.
- Continue listening with the screen locked.
- Feel gentle rhythmic haptics on supported iPhones while the app is active. iOS pauses haptics when the app goes into the background.

## Private and simple

PurrHeart works offline, with no account, advertising, analytics or tracking. It does not access the microphone, camera, location or HealthKit. A playback history is stored locally on your device and is not sent to the developer. External websites and wallet services have their own privacy practices.

The app is for quiet listening and personal comfort. It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
